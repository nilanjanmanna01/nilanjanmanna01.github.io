As a hobby, I explore wearables, health tech, machine learning, and
whatever else catches my interest. This site is where I write down what I try and what
I learn.

I have been inspired by many different kinds of content that manage to teach
complex topics in a simple way. Along the way I realized there is plenty of
room to explain how things are actually calculated, rather than glossing
over them.

I am not an expert in any of these fields. All of my articles are a
reflection of my own interpretation, and I would love to hear from people
who are more knowledgeable in these areas.
